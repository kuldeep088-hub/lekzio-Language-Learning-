Core Features

Six essential tools covering the fundamental pillars of language learning.

💬
AI Conversation Partner
Chat in real-time with an AI native speaker calibrated to your exact CEFR level. Powered by streaming Server-Sent Events for instant responses. Choose your correction mode — Gentle (end of conversation), Strict (inline corrections), or Silent (full immersion). Pick from 20+ topic categories: travel, business, culture, food, current events, and more.
Streaming SSECEFR Adaptive
✏️
Grammar Checker
Deep linguistic analysis beyond spellcheck. Every error is tagged with its grammar rule name, severity level (minor / moderate / critical), a plain-English explanation, and a suggested correction. Your full text receives an overall score from 0–100. Supports all 10 languages with language-specific rule engines and grammar models.
Rule Engine10 Languages
📚
Vocabulary Flashcards
AI-generated flashcards with IPA phonetic transcription, part of speech, 3 mnemonic devices, top collocations, 5 example sentences graded A1–C2, and cultural usage notes. The SM-2 spaced repetition algorithm determines optimal review intervals — so you review words right before you would otherwise forget them.
SM-2 SRSIPA Phonetics
🎓
Lesson Generator
Choose your language, CEFR target level, and topic. Claude generates a complete lesson: a 20-word vocabulary list, 5 grammar rules with examples, a 300-word reading passage, 10 comprehension questions, and fill-in-the-blank exercises that are auto-graded instantly. Every lesson is unique — you will never see the same lesson twice.
Dynamic ContentAuto-Graded
🌐
Translation with Context
Go far beyond word-for-word translation. Get a literal word-by-word breakdown, cultural context notes, a formality level indicator (casual / neutral / formal / very formal), three alternative phrasings in different registers, and an analysis of connotations and regional variations across countries where the language is spoken.
Cultural AIRegister Analysis
⚡
Daily Challenges
Five rotating challenge types refresh every 24 hours: Spot the Error (find grammar mistakes in a passage), Word of the Day (learn in context), Translation Sprint (10 phrases in 3 minutes), Dialogue Completion (fill missing lines in a conversation), and Synonym Chain (find 5 synonyms with nuance differences). Complete all five to earn your daily XP bonus.
Daily XP5 Types
