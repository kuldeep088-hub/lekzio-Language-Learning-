ABOUT LEKZIO

About Lekzio

Built by language learners, powered by AI. We believe fluency should be accessible to everyone.

OUR MISSION

Making Fluency Accessible to Everyone

Our mission is to make fluency accessible to everyone through the power of artificial intelligence. Language learning has historically been expensive, intimidating, and slow — gated behind classroom schedules, expensive tutors, and textbooks that go out of date the moment they are printed.

We believe that everyone deserves a world-class language tutor — one that is available 24 hours a day, speaks to you at exactly your level, corrects you without judgment, and adapts to your unique learning style. That is what Lekzio is building: not a gamified distraction, but a genuine learning companion powered by Claude AI.

THE STORY

Why We Built Lekzio

We were frustrated by the limitations of existing language apps. Pre-scripted lessons that never deviate. Gamification without depth. No real conversation — just tapping the right word at the right time. After years of trying Duolingo, Babbel, and expensive tutors, something was always missing: genuine, adaptive intelligence.

When Claude AI became available, the potential was immediately clear. Here was an AI that could hold a real conversation, explain grammar rules in plain language, generate fresh lessons on any topic, and correct with nuance and cultural awareness. We spent six months building Lekzio from scratch — a FastAPI backend, a carefully designed prompt architecture, and a feature set grounded in actual linguistics research.

Every feature in Lekzio exists because a language learner needed it. Not because it looked good in a product demo.

TECHNOLOGY

Built on Solid Foundations

A modern, scalable stack chosen for performance, reliability, and developer experience.

⚡
FastAPI
High-performance Python backend with automatic OpenAPI docs and async support for SSE streaming.
🧠
Claude AI
Anthropic's Claude powers all 30+ AI features — conversation, grammar, lessons, stories, shadowing analysis, skill radar, CEFR testing, and more.
⚛️
React
Modern component-based frontend with hooks, context, and real-time UI updates via SSE.
🗄️
SQLite
Lightweight relational database for user data, vocabulary decks, XP history, and progress tracking.
📡
SSE Streaming
Server-Sent Events deliver Claude's responses token-by-token for a natural, real-time conversation feel.
🧮
SM-2 Algorithm
The scientifically-validated spaced repetition algorithm used in Anki, applied to vocabulary flashcards.
📊
Skill Radar Analytics
Session-by-session proficiency tracking across 6 dimensions — Reading, Writing, Speaking, Listening, Vocabulary, and Grammar — rendered as an interactive radar chart.
🧬
Ebbinghaus Forgetting Curve
Memory science model that predicts when learners are about to forget vocabulary or grammar rules, proactively scheduling micro-reviews before knowledge degrades.

OUR VALUES

What We Stand For

🎯
Depth over Gamification
We do not believe in reducing language learning to streak notifications and cartoon owls. Real progress comes from deliberate practice, genuine conversation, and understanding grammar rules — not from collecting badges.
🤖
Real AI, Not Scripted
Every response is generated fresh by Claude AI. There are no decision trees, no pre-written scripts, no if-then logic masquerading as intelligence. When you have a conversation with Lekzio, you are talking to actual AI.
📈
Adaptive Learning
Your Lekzio experience is personalized to you, not to an average learner. CEFR levels, correction modes, lesson topics, and review schedules all adapt based on your actual performance and preferences.
🌍
Accessible to All
A meaningful Free plan, no paywalls on the core experience, and fair pricing for students and individuals. Language learning should not cost as much per month as a language class per hour.
