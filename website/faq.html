⚡Features
The AI Conversation feature uses Claude to simulate a native speaker conversation partner calibrated to your exact CEFR level. Responses stream in real-time via Server-Sent Events (SSE) so they appear character by character, just like a real chat. You choose your correction mode: Gentle (corrections at the end), Strict (inline during the conversation), or Silent (full immersion). You can also select from 20+ topic categories.
Lekzio supports Spanish, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Mandarin Chinese, Portuguese, and Arabic at launch. These eight languages cover the most commonly studied languages worldwide. Additional languages are planned for future updates based on user demand. Sign up and let us know what language you want to see added next.
The Pronunciation Coach uses your browser's built-in microphone — no plugins or downloads required. You record yourself saying a target phrase, and the AI compares your pronunciation against native speaker patterns. You receive an IPA-based breakdown showing which phonemes were correct, close, or off, along with specific tips for each sound you need to improve.
Interactive Story Mode puts you inside branching narratives written entirely in your target language. Choose from 5 genres: Adventure, Mystery, Romance, Sci-Fi, and Historical Fiction. At key decision points in the story, you choose what happens next — and the story branches accordingly. Vocabulary is calibrated to your CEFR level. Tap any word to see its translation without leaving the story.
Lekzio uses the SM-2 algorithm — the same algorithm used in Anki, the world's most popular spaced repetition tool. After each flashcard review, you rate how well you remembered the word. SM-2 uses this rating to calculate exactly when you should see that word again: soon if you struggled, much later if you knew it well. This ensures you review words at the optimal moment to maximize long-term retention with minimum study time.
Daily Challenges are five short exercises that refresh every 24 hours: Spot the Error (find grammar mistakes in a passage), Word of the Day (learn a new word in context), Translation Sprint (translate 10 phrases against a timer), Dialogue Completion (fill in the missing lines of a conversation), and Synonym Chain (find 5 synonyms with nuance differences). Completing all five earns your daily XP bonus and extends your learning streak.
Shadowing is an elite language learning technique used by polyglots worldwide. In Lekzio's Shadowing Mode, the AI speaks a sentence and you repeat it. Our AI then analyzes your timing, rhythm, and intonation and gives you a detailed score with specific phonetic tips.
After each session, Lekzio measures your performance across 6 dimensions: Reading, Writing, Speaking, Listening, Vocabulary, and Grammar. These scores update your personal Skill Radar Chart — a hexagonal visual map that instantly shows your strengths and areas to improve.
The CEFR Level Test is a formal 20-question adaptive assessment that covers all skill areas and places you accurately on the A1 to C2 scale. You can take it at any time to measure your progress. Upon completion, you receive a detailed score breakdown and a downloadable digital Level Certificate.
